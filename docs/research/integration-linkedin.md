# LinkedIn - obtaining the user's OWN job-application and recruiter-message data WITHOUT writing anything to LinkedIn

## Summary
There is no official LinkedIn API through which a general developer can read a member's applied jobs, saved jobs, messages/InMail, or run job search: the only self-serve products are "Sign In with LinkedIn using OpenID Connect" (scopes openid/profile/email, returns only name, picture, email, locale via https://api.linkedin.com/v2/userinfo) and "Share on LinkedIn" (w_member_social, a write scope JobPilot must never request); Talent Solutions programs (Recruiter System Connect, Apply Connect, Apply with LinkedIn, Premium Job Posting) are partner-only for incorporated ATS/HR vendors, the Job Posting API is closed to new partners, and the Compliance API (r_compliance) is closed entirely. The two fully compliant, read-only sources of the user's own data are (1) the official "Get a copy of your data" archive (Settings & Privacy > Data privacy > Get a copy of your data), where the Jobs tier (Job Applications, Saved Jobs, Job Seeker Preferences, Saved Job Alerts, Invitations, Profile, Positions, Skills) arrives in about 10 minutes and the full archive (Messages, Connections, Inferences, Search Queries) within about 24 hours, with a 72-hour download link; and (2) LinkedIn's own notification emails in the user's Gmail (application updates, message/InMail notices, job alerts), which are near-real-time and can be read with the gmail.readonly scope. Both are permitted by design; neither writes to LinkedIn. Community parsers document the archive schemas: Jobs/Job Applications.csv (Application Date, Contact Email, Contact Phone Number, Job Title, Company Name, Job Url, Resume Name, Question And Answers, and in newer exports possibly Status and Withdraw Date), Jobs/Saved Jobs.csv (Saved Date, Company Name, Job Title, Job Url), Jobs/Job Seeker Preferences.csv (Locations, Industries, Preferred Job Types, Job Titles, Open To Recruiters, Dream Companies, Profile Shared With Job Poster, Introduction Statement, Phone Number), and messages.csv (CONVERSATION ID, CONVERSATION TITLE, FROM, SENDER PROFILE URL, TO, RECIPIENT PROFILE URLS, DATE, SUBJECT, CONTENT, FOLDER, ATTACHMENTS, IS MESSAGE DRAFT). Key limitations: LinkedIn only tracks Easy Apply applications plus external applications the user confirms via the "Did you apply?" prompt; "Viewed / Resume downloaded / Rejected" updates are only emitted when the employer enables applicant notifications; and message-notification emails historically contain only the sender and a "View message" button, so full recruiter message text is available only from messages.csv. Every automated alternative (Playwright on a logged-in Chrome profile, li_at cookie reuse against the internal Voyager API, Unipile/Linked API/Phantombuster, and even job-tracker browser extensions) violates User Agreement Section 8.2 (effective November 3, 2025), which bans scrapers, bots, browser plugins/add-ons and copying data from the Services; LinkedIn Help a1341387 states violators "risk having their accounts restricted or shut down", and read-only intent is not a defense. The 2025-2026 record (Proxycurl shut down July 2025 under permanent injunction; ProAPIs sued October 2025 and settling February 2026; hiQ's 2022 consent judgment after LinkedIn won breach-of-contract summary judgment; Meta v. Bright Data protecting only logged-off public scraping by non-users) confirms that logged-in automation is the enforced bright line. Recommendation: v1 = Gmail read-only ingestion of LinkedIn notification emails for real-time signals + a repeatable manual archive-import step for ground truth + a manual/voice "log application" command for external ATS applications; defer all browser automation indefinitely for the user's real account.

## Hard constraints
  - No official LinkedIn API exposes a member's applied jobs, saved jobs, messages, InMail, or job search to general developers. Self-serve 'Open Permissions' are only: profile, email (Sign In with LinkedIn using OpenID Connect) and w_member_social (Share on LinkedIn). Everything else requires partner approval (learn.microsoft.com/linkedin/shared/authentication/getting-access).
  - Talent Solutions APIs (Recruiter System Connect, Apply Connect, Apply with LinkedIn, Premium Job Posting) are for approved ATS/HR-tech partner companies via business.linkedin.com/talent-solutions/ats-partners/partner-application; the Job Posting API is 'currently not accepting new partnerships'; the Compliance API (r_compliance/w_compliance) is closed and 'may not be requested'.
  - w_member_social is a WRITE scope (post/comment/like). JobPilot must never request it; the OIDC scopes openid/profile/email are the only ones compatible with the read-only constraint.
  - LinkedIn User Agreement Section 8.2 (effective November 3, 2025) prohibits: (2) developing/using 'software, devices, scripts, robots or any other means or processes (such as crawlers, browser plugins and add-ons or any other technology) to scrape or copy the Services'; (3) bypassing access controls or use limits; (4) copying/using information obtained from the Services without consent; (13) using 'bots or other unauthorized automated methods to access the Services, add or download contacts, send or redirect messages'. LinkedIn Help a1341387 ('Prohibited software and extensions') adds that violators 'risk having their accounts restricted or shut down'. Read-only intent is not an exception.
  - Sign In with LinkedIn v1 scopes (r_liteprofile, r_emailaddress) are retired; only OIDC (openid, profile, email) is listed as self-serve in 2026.
  - 'Get a copy of your data' is desktop-web only, requires manual clicks, tiers data: ~10 minutes for Job Applications, Saved Jobs, Job Seeker Preferences, Saved Job Alerts, Job Application Saved Answers, Job Applicant Saved Screening Question Responses, Invitations, Profile, Positions, Skills, Education, Recommendations, etc.; ~24 hours (page says 24h; commonly 24-72h) for Messages, Connections, Email Addresses, Inferences, Search Queries, Logins, Comments, Shares, Reactions, Saved Items. Download link expires after 72 hours. A new archive can reportedly be requested only every ~2 hours and not while one is pending (from LinkedIn's email text as reported by third parties; not stated on the help page).
  - LinkedIn's Applied list (Jobs > Job tracker > Applied) and therefore Job Applications.csv only include Easy Apply applications plus external applications the user explicitly confirmed via the 'Did you apply?' prompt (or manually added). Applications completed on company ATS sites are otherwise invisible to LinkedIn.
  - Applicant status updates exist only as Application Viewed, Resume Downloaded, Application Rejected (Apply Connect docs), and only if the employer opted in to applicant notifications; LinkedIn Help a508716 confirms 'viewed' and 'resume downloaded' updates for Easy Apply jobs hosted on LinkedIn. There is no 'interview scheduled' or 'offer' signal from LinkedIn.
  - LinkedIn message-notification emails have historically contained only the sender's name/headline and a 'View message' button, not the message body; the full text of recruiter messages/InMails is only available in messages.csv from the full archive.
  - Archive CSV schemas are undocumented by LinkedIn and change between export versions; files may be split (Job Applications_1.csv) or suffixed with the member id (Shares_506219023.csv); parsers must read the header row dynamically and match file names by normalized stem, not exact string.
  - hiQ v. LinkedIn ended (Nov-Dec 2022) with summary judgment for LinkedIn on breach of contract and a stipulated USD 500,000 judgment plus permanent injunction; the Ninth Circuit's CFAA holding protects only scraping of PUBLIC pages, not logged-in access. Proxycurl (LinkedIn v. Nubela, 3:25-cv-00828, filed Jan 24 2025) shut down July 4 2025 under a permanent injunction; ProAPIs (5:25-cv-08393, filed Oct 2 2025) reached a settlement in principle Feb 2026. Meta v. Bright Data (Jan 23 2024) protects logged-off public scraping by non-users only. Any logged-in automated access to LinkedIn is the enforced bright line.

## Recommendation
v1 (build now, all read-only and ToS-clean): (1) Gmail ingestion of LinkedIn notification emails as the near-real-time signal: gmail.readonly scope, poll `from:(@linkedin.com) newer_than:2d` (plus @e.linkedin.com / @el.linkedin.com), classify by sender+subject template into li_email_event, extract li_job_id from /comm/jobs/view/(\\d+) links, and turn on the LinkedIn email toggles for Job application updates (Individual), Job alerts (Daily), Job recommendations, and Conversations/Messages. Add a one-time 'sender discovery' routine that lists distinct LinkedIn sender addresses and subject templates in the user's mailbox (the Gmail MCP was not connected in this session so exact addresses could not be confirmed). (2) A repeatable manual archive import: an in-app checklist that opens https://www.linkedin.com/mypreferences/d/download-my-data, tells the user which tier to request (Jobs categories for a 10-minute refresh; full archive weekly/bi-weekly for messages.csv), then a Downloads-folder watcher / `import linkedin <zip>` command that stores the raw ZIP, parses Jobs/Job Applications.csv, Jobs/Saved Jobs.csv, Jobs/Job Seeker Preferences.csv, Jobs/SavedJobAlerts.csv, messages.csv, Connections.csv, Profile.csv, Positions.csv, Skills.csv, and upserts idempotently by natural keys, recording provenance (source_import_id) per row. Show 'LinkedIn data as of <archive date>' in the UI. (3) A manual/voice 'log application' intent plus a nudge to always answer 'Yes' to LinkedIn's 'Did you apply?' prompt so external applications enter LinkedIn's own tracker and future exports. (4) Optionally the OIDC sign-in only to capture a stable `sub`/email for the profile record; do not request w_member_social. Defer to v2+: parsing messages.csv into recruiter conversation threads with LLM summarization; scheduled reminders ('archive is 14 days old, request a new one'); ATS-email parsing for non-LinkedIn applications; and, only if the user explicitly accepts the risk, a feature-flagged logged-out single-page fetch of pasted job URLs for description enrichment. Do NOT build Playwright/li_at/Voyager automation or use Unipile/Linked API/Phantombuster against the user's real account: it violates Section 8.2, LinkedIn detects it, and account restriction during an active job search is an unacceptable failure mode; a 'throwaway' account is itself a fake-account violation and would not contain the user's data anyway.

## Options
### Official data archive import ('Get a copy of your data') [read_only_ok=True, cost=Free, effort=days, risk=none]
User requests the archive at Settings & Privacy > Data privacy > Get a copy of your data (desktop only). Choose 'specific data' with Jobs categories for a ~10-minute export, or the full archive (~24h, sometimes 48-72h) when messages.csv/Connections.csv are needed. LinkedIn emails a link valid 72 hours; ZIP is named Basic_LinkedInDataExport_MM-DD-YYYY.zip or Complete_LinkedInDataExport_MM-DD-YYYY.zip. JobPilot watches %USERPROFILE%\Downloads for *_LinkedInDataExport_*.zip (or exposes `jobpilot import linkedin <zip>`), archives the raw ZIP, parses the CSVs with csv.DictReader (multi-line quoted fields), and upserts by natural keys (Job Url + Application Date; CONVERSATION ID + DATE + SENDER PROFILE URL).
Pros:
  - 100% compliant: LinkedIn's own GDPR/CCPA export mechanism; zero writes
  - Only source of full recruiter message text (messages.csv CONTENT) and of Saved Jobs, Job Seeker Preferences, Saved Job Alerts, Invitations
  - Rich, structured CSVs with stable-ish schemas; community parsers exist (linkedin2md, linkedin-csv.rs, LinkedIn-Data-Formatter)
  - Also yields Profile/Positions/Skills for the memory/profile system and Inferences_about_you.csv / SearchQueries.csv for preference mining
  - Free; no API keys; no partner approval
Cons:
  - Manual, not real-time: user must click 'Request archive' each time; ~10 min (Jobs tier) to 24-72 h (full archive); re-request reportedly allowed only every ~2 hours and not while one is pending
  - Desktop web only (not mobile)
  - Schema undocumented by LinkedIn and changes over time; files may be split (_1/_2) or suffixed with member id; must read header row dynamically
  - Job Applications.csv only contains Easy Apply applications and external applications confirmed via 'Did you apply?'
  - Status/outcome fields are sparse (Status/Withdraw Date only in newer exports, unverified); no 'viewed/rejected' history
### Gmail read-only ingestion of LinkedIn notification emails [read_only_ok=True, cost=Free (Gmail API quota is ample for one user), effort=days, risk=none]
Use Gmail API scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly (or Gmail MCP search_threads) to poll `from:(@linkedin.com) newer_than:Nd` (optionally also `from:(@e.linkedin.com OR @el.linkedin.com)`), classify each message by sender + subject template into event types (application_sent, application_viewed, resume_downloaded, application_rejected, message_received/inmail_received, job_alert, job_recommendation, invitation), extract company/title and the job id from links matching https://www.linkedin.com/comm/jobs/view/(\d+), and store as li_email_event rows linked to job_posting/application. Requires the user to turn ON email for Settings & Privacy > Notifications > 'Searching for a job' > Job application updates (frequency Individual, not Weekly), Job alerts (Jobs > Preferences > Job alerts, Daily), Job recommendations, and Messaging/Conversations email notifications.
Pros:
  - Compliant: reading mail LinkedIn sent to the user; LinkedIn cannot observe it; no writes
  - Near-real-time (minutes) tracking of application sent/viewed/resume-downloaded/rejected and of new recruiter messages
  - Same Gmail pipeline also captures ATS confirmations (Greenhouse, Workday, Lever) for external applications LinkedIn never sees
  - Gmail API is stable, well documented, and already planned for JobPilot
Cons:
  - Message-notification emails historically contain only sender name/headline and a 'View message' button, not the message body; InMail emails may include a preview but not reliably
  - Status emails depend on the employer having enabled applicant notifications; many applications never get a 'viewed' email
  - Sender addresses/subject templates are not officially documented; classification must be template-based and tolerant (LinkedIn changes templates; localized subjects if UI language is Japanese)
  - Email links carry tracking params (trk, lipi, midToken, otpToken) that must be stripped; job alert emails are multipart MIME with the plain-text part easiest to parse
### Official LinkedIn API (Sign In with LinkedIn using OpenID Connect) as identity anchor only [read_only_ok=True, cost=Free, effort=hours, risk=none]
Create an app in the LinkedIn Developer Portal, add the self-serve product 'Sign in with LinkedIn using OpenID Connect', run the 3-legged code flow (authorization_endpoint https://www.linkedin.com/oauth/v2/authorization, token_endpoint https://www.linkedin.com/oauth/v2/accessToken, scopes openid profile email), call GET https://api.linkedin.com/v2/userinfo to obtain sub, name, given_name, family_name, picture, locale, email. Do NOT add 'Share on LinkedIn' (w_member_social) because it is a write scope.
Pros:
  - Fully official and self-serve; no partner approval
  - Provides a stable member identifier (sub) and verified email to anchor the profile record
Cons:
  - Returns nothing about jobs, applications, saved jobs, messages, or search; zero value for tracking
  - Requires a registered LinkedIn app with a redirect URI; overkill for a single-user local app
  - Partner programs (RSC, Apply Connect, Job Posting, SNAP, Compliance) are unavailable to individuals; Job Posting closed to new partners
### Manual/voice 'log application' command plus 'Did you apply?' discipline [read_only_ok=True, cost=Free, effort=hours, risk=none]
In JobPilot, a chat/voice intent like 'I just applied to X at Y' or pasting a LinkedIn job URL creates an application row (channel=external_ats or linkedin_easy_apply) and a job_posting row keyed by the numeric id in https://www.linkedin.com/jobs/view/{id}. Separately, the user always clicks 'Yes' on LinkedIn's 'Did you apply?' prompt after external applications so they also appear in LinkedIn's Job tracker > Applied tab and in Job Applications.csv.
Pros:
  - Compliant; covers the large gap of external-ATS applications that LinkedIn never records
  - Immediate; fits the required voice-command feature
  - Makes later archive imports reconcile cleanly (same Job Url key)
Cons:
  - Relies on user discipline; no automation
  - Job details (description, salary) must be pasted or captured from the confirmation email
### Logged-out fetch of individual public job pages the user pastes [read_only_ok=True, cost=Free, effort=hours, risk=low]
For enrichment only: when the user pastes https://www.linkedin.com/jobs/view/{id}, JobPilot performs a single unauthenticated HTTP GET (no cookies) to read the public job description/company/location, with caching and a hard per-day cap.
Pros:
  - No account session involved, so no account-restriction vector
  - Meta v. Bright Data (N.D. Cal., Jan 23 2024) held logged-off scraping of public data by a non-user is not bound by the platform ToS
Cons:
  - The user IS a LinkedIn member bound by Section 8.2 clauses 2 and 4 (scrape/copy 'the Services'), so this is still a technical ToS breach even if practically unenforceable at one page at a time
  - LinkedIn aggressively auth-walls and rate-limits guest job pages (redirects to login, HTTP 429/999), so reliability is poor
  - Adds legal grey area to an otherwise clean architecture for modest benefit; the same data can be pasted by the user
### Job-seeker browser extensions (Teal, Huntr, Simplify) as a side channel [read_only_ok=True, cost=Free tiers; Teal+ ~USD 9-29/mo for premium, effort=hours, risk=medium]
User installs a mainstream job-tracker extension that saves LinkedIn job pages into its own board; JobPilot imports that tool's CSV export. JobPilot itself does not touch LinkedIn.
Pros:
  - Low effort for the user; widely used; no documented LinkedIn enforcement against these specific tools
  - Captures job details at save/apply time
Cons:
  - Technically prohibited by Section 8.2 / Help a1341387 ('browser plug-ins or browser extensions that scrape, modify the appearance of, or automate activity')
  - Data goes to a third-party SaaS; JobPilot only gets it via their export, not an API
  - Does not provide messages or application status
### Browser automation on the user's logged-in session (Playwright + Chrome profile, or li_at + JSESSIONID against https://www.linkedin.com/voyager/api) [read_only_ok=True, cost=Free (engineering time + maintenance), effort=weeks, risk=high]
Drive the user's real session to read Jobs > Job tracker > Applied/Saved and Messaging, or call internal Voyager endpoints via libraries like `linkedin-api` (PyPI). Read-only in effect, but automated access to the authenticated site.
Pros:
  - Would give near-real-time applied/saved lists and full message bodies
  - Open-source tooling exists (linkedin-api wraps Voyager: profile, search_jobs, get_job, conversations)
Cons:
  - Direct violation of User Agreement 8.2 (clauses 2, 3, 4, 13) regardless of read-only intent; LinkedIn Help a1341387: accounts 'restricted or shut down'
  - LinkedIn actively fingerprints automation and extensions (it just defeated privacy suits over its anti-scraping browser checks, Sept 2026); Voyager issues CHALLENGE after ~900 requests/hour and 2FA accounts trigger challenges; false-positive restrictions are reported even for humans
  - Losing the account mid-job-search is catastrophic for the user's actual goal; recovery is opaque (noreply emails pointing to the prohibited-software page)
  - Fragile: undocumented endpoints and DOM change without notice
  - Every logged-in scraper LinkedIn has sued (hiQ, Proxycurl, ProAPIs) ended with a judgment/injunction; personal use is unlikely to be litigated but account enforcement is automatic
### Unofficial 'own account' API vendors (Unipile, Linked API, Phantombuster) [read_only_ok=True, cost=USD ~50-150/month, effort=hours, risk=high]
Hand the vendor the user's LinkedIn credentials or li_at cookie; they run a reverse-engineered Voyager client or cloud browser and expose REST endpoints for inbox, profile, jobs.
Pros:
  - Fast integration; hosted; handles proxies and session churn
  - Read endpoints exist for conversations and profile
Cons:
  - All state they are 'not affiliated with LinkedIn' and operate 'through reverse engineering'; same 8.2 violation plus credential/cookie sharing with a third party (LinkedIn shows a credential-sharing warning on Unipile login)
  - Vendors' own docs: 'no guarantee against restriction', 'treat any LinkedIn account used ... as expendable'
  - Paid (Unipile ~USD 55+/account/month class pricing; Phantombuster from ~USD 69/month)
  - Proxycurl, the largest such provider, was sued Jan 2025 and shut down July 2025

## Concrete details
  - OIDC (identity only): authorization_endpoint https://www.linkedin.com/oauth/v2/authorization ; token_endpoint https://www.linkedin.com/oauth/v2/accessToken ; userinfo GET https://api.linkedin.com/v2/userinfo (Bearer) ; jwks https://www.linkedin.com/oauth/openid/jwks ; discovery https://www.linkedin.com/oauth/.well-known/openid-configuration ; scopes openid profile email ; response fields sub, name, given_name, family_name, picture, locale, email (optional), email_verified (optional). Never request w_member_social.
  - Data export UI path: Me > Settings & Privacy > Data privacy > 'Get a copy of your data' (direct URL https://www.linkedin.com/mypreferences/d/download-my-data). Option A 'specific data' (choose Job Applications, Saved Jobs, Job Seeker Preferences, Saved Job Alerts, Job Application Saved Answers, Job Applicant Saved Screening Question Responses, Invitations, Profile, Positions, Skills...) -> email within ~10 minutes. Option B 'larger download' (full archive incl. Messages, Connections, Inferences, Search Queries) -> email within ~24 h (two emails: partial then complete). Link valid 72 h. 'Request new archive' link appears after completion; ~2 h cooldown reported. Desktop only.
  - Archive ZIP names: Basic_LinkedInDataExport_MM-DD-YYYY.zip (specific data) and Complete_LinkedInDataExport_MM-DD-YYYY.zip (full). Job files live under a Jobs/ subfolder. Files may be split into ..._1.csv/_2.csv and some files carry a member-id suffix (e.g. Shares_506219023.csv); match by regex on normalized stem: key = Path(name).stem.lower().replace(' ', '_') with optional _\d+ suffix stripped.
  - Jobs/Job Applications.csv header (from linkedin2md parser; verify on first export): Application Date, Contact Email, Contact Phone Number, Job Title, Company Name, Job Url, Resume Name, Question And Answers, [Status], [Withdraw Date]. 'Application Date' observed format like '6/11/17, 10:19 PM' (M/D/YY, h:mm AM/PM) in older exports; parse leniently. Job Url pattern https://www.linkedin.com/jobs/view/{numeric id}/ -> extract li_job_id with /jobs/view/(\d+).
  - Jobs/Saved Jobs.csv header: Saved Date, Company Name, Job Title, Job Url.
  - Jobs/Job Seeker Preferences.csv header: Locations, Industries, Preferred Job Types, Job Titles, Open To Recruiters, Dream Companies, Profile Shared With Job Poster, Introduction Statement, Phone Number (single-row snapshot; feed into the memory/profile system as 'declared preferences').
  - Jobs/SavedJobAlerts.csv header: SAVED_SEARCH_ID, ALERT_PARAMETERS, QUERY_CONTEXT (previous searches/alerts; useful for requirement 4 'previously searched results').
  - Jobs/Job Applicant Saved Answers.csv header: Question, Answer. Jobs/Job Applicant Saved Screening Question Responses.csv: per-application screening answers (header varies).
  - messages.csv header: CONVERSATION ID, CONVERSATION TITLE, FROM, SENDER PROFILE URL, TO, RECIPIENT PROFILE URLS, DATE, SUBJECT, CONTENT, FOLDER, ATTACHMENTS, IS MESSAGE DRAFT (some versions add IS CONVERSATION DRAFT). CONTENT is multi-line; use csv.DictReader(open(path, newline='', encoding='utf-8-sig')). FOLDER distinguishes INBOX/ARCHIVE etc. Recruiter InMails appear as ordinary conversations; identify recruiters heuristically (SENDER PROFILE URL not in Connections.csv, subject present, keywords).
  - Connections.csv header: First Name, Last Name, URL, Email Address, Company, Position, Connected On (note: file begins with a 3-line 'Notes:' preamble before the header in many exports; skip until the header row). Invitations.csv: From, To, Sent At, Message, Direction, inviterProfileUrl, inviteeProfileUrl. Profile.csv: First Name, Last Name, Maiden Name, Address, Birth Date, Headline, Summary, Industry, Zip Code, Geo Location, Twitter Handles, Websites, Instant Messengers. Positions.csv: Company Name, Title, Description, Location, Started On, Finished On. Skills.csv: Name. SearchQueries.csv: Time, Search Query. Inferences_about_you.csv: LinkedIn's inferred traits (e.g. job-search interest).
  - Gmail source: scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly ; base query `from:(@linkedin.com) newer_than:2d` ; bulk subdomains @e.linkedin.com and @el.linkedin.com also carry alerts/campaigns. Known sender addresses (third-party-confirmed): messages-noreply@linkedin.com (message notices), notifications-noreply@linkedin.com, jobalerts-noreply@linkedin.com (job alerts), jobs-listings@linkedin.com (job recommendations/listings), invitations@linkedin.com, inmail-hit-reply@linkedin.com (InMail; reply-to at reply.linkedin.com), hit-reply@linkedin.com, updates-noreply@linkedin.com, security-noreply@linkedin.com, linkedin@e.linkedin.com, linkedin@el.linkedin.com. Widely reported but NOT confirmed by an indexed source: jobs-noreply@linkedin.com for 'Your application was sent to {Company}' / 'Your application was viewed by {Company}'. Implement sender discovery from the user's own mailbox rather than hardcoding.
  - Email parsing: job alert and application emails are multipart MIME; parse the text/plain part; job links look like https://www.linkedin.com/comm/jobs/view/{id}?trk=...&lipi=...&midToken=...&otpToken=... -> strip query string, keep id. Message links look like https://www.linkedin.com/comm/messaging/thread/{urn}. Every genuine LinkedIn email has a security footer with the member's name and headline; verify DKIM/SPF via Gmail headers (Authentication-Results) before trusting.
  - LinkedIn settings the user must enable: Settings & Privacy > Notifications > 'Searching for a job' > Job application updates (channels In-app/Push/Email; set Email ON and frequency Individual), Job alerts, Job recommendations; Jobs > Preferences > Job alerts (max 20 alerts; Daily or Weekly; email + in-app); Settings & Privacy > Notifications > Messaging/Conversations > Email ON; Jobs > Preferences > Open to work (fields: Job titles, Location types, Locations, Start date, Employment types; visibility All members / Recruiters only / Only you). Jobs > Job tracker > Applied tab shows Applied / Viewed / Resume downloaded; always answer 'Yes' to 'Did you apply?' after external applies.
