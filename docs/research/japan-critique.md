## Overall
The research is broad and mostly accurate on the regulatory timeline (内閣官房 R8.3.24 要請 with 3/1, 6/1, 10/1 dates confirmed), navi-site sender addresses (マイナビ list confirmed verbatim), the ATS layer (Greenhouse, Ashby, Lever, Workable widget JSON, Sony AI Workday tenant, sonar/i-web URL patterns all confirmed), and visa timing (ISA 12/1-1/31 window, 2025-12-01 document simplification, and the 2026-04-15 Category-3/4 rules all confirmed on official pages). The most consequential error is Rakuten: the 'October 2027 Entry' is Business-track only, deadline 2026-09-29 08:59 JST, hosted on i-web (mypage.3030.i-webs.jp) rather than Workday, and the Engineer 2027 intake is closed - so the one 'open big-tech 新卒 window' the summary leans on is far narrower than stated for an AI/ML candidate. Several sender-domain rules presented as verified are not (HERP candidacy.herp.cloud FAQ 404s, リクナビ help article is JS-only, マイナビ job-gift address not on the cited page), and Ashby's default is no-reply@ (hyphen). The 'MyPage still live = window open' inference is unreliable: Toyota is confirmed open for 27卒 (応募 button, eligibility 2023-04 to 2027-03 grads, 博士 via キャリア採用), but DeNA shows only 受付終了 entries and CyberAgent advertises 2028 only. Important gaps: Workable's workablemail.com sender domain (needed for Mercari/SmartNews), late-cycle 理系 scout channels (ABABA, アカリク, dodaキャンパス), 留学生 channels (東京外国人雇用サービスセンター 11/17 面接会), and the ISA 特定活動 46号 / 内定待機 pages that directly enable an October 2027 start. Snapshot counts (Sakana 23 -> 27 roles, Mercari 324 -> 240 jobs) confirm the app must store these with timestamps rather than as facts.
